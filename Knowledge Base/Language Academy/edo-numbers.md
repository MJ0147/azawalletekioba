# Edo Numbers 1–100

Counting from one to a hundred in Edo (Bini), for the EKIOBA Edo Language Academy. The numbers
1–10 come from a printed numbers chart, 11–30 from two photos of handwritten notes, and 31–100
from a typed list. All were supplied by the project owner, who also confirmed that 1 is *owo* or
*ọkpa*.

## Patterns in this list

These are patterns visible in the list itself; check them with a native speaker before teaching
them as rules.

- **12–14** are *iw-* followed by 2, 3 and 4: *iw-eva*, *iw-eha*, *iw-ene*.
- **16–19** count down from 20 (*ugie*): they begin with 4, 3, 2 and 1 (*enẹirrọvbugie*,
  *ehairrọvbugie*, *evairrọvbugie*, *ọkpairrọvbugie*), each ending in *-irrọvbugie*.
- **21–25** count up from 20: *ọkpa* (1) to *isèn* (5), each followed by *yan ugie*.
- **26–29** count down from 30 (*ọgban*) the same way, each ending in *-irrọvbọgban*.
- **40, 60, 80 and 100 count in twenties.** Each is *iy-* followed by 2, 3, 4 or 5: *iyeva* (40),
  *iyeha* (60), *iyenen* (80) and *iyisen* (100).
- **50, 70 and 90** each contain the next of those: *ekigbesiyeha* (50) contains *iyeha* (60),
  *ekigbesiyenen* (70) contains *iyenen* (80), and *ekigbesiyisen* (90) contains *iyisen* (100).
- **From 31, every decade works like 21–30.** *X yan* followed by the base adds 1–5
  (*ọkpa yan ọgban*, 31). *X ẹi rrọ vbe* followed by the next base counts down 4–1
  (*enen ẹi rrọ vbe iyeva*, 36, is four short of 40).

## Notes

- **Handwriting.** 11–30 are handwritten. The second form of 15 is read as *ekesugie*; check it.
- **Ditto marks.** 23 and 24 were written as "Eha 〃 〃" and "Enẹ 〃 〃" and are written out in
  full here.
- **Spelling.** The project owner chose *enen* for 4, *isèn* for 5 and *yan* (plain *y*) for
  "and". They are used for 4 and 5 on their own and in every number written as separate words
  (21–25, 34, 35, 44, 45 and so on). The sources had also written *ene*, *enẹ*, *isen*, *isẹn*,
  and *ỹan* in the handwritten 21–25.
- **Kept as supplied.** The one-word forms for 16 and 26 (*enẹirrọvbugie*, *enẹirrọvbọgban*), where
  the *ẹ* seems to belong to *ẹi* as in the typed *enen ẹi rrọ vbe*; and *iyisen* (100) and
  *ekigbesiyisen* (90), which contain 5 inside a single word.
- **Word spacing.** 36 and above write count-down numbers as separate words (*enen ẹi rrọ vbe
  iyeva*, 36); the handwritten 16–19 and 26–29 run them together (*enẹirrọvbugie*).
- **English.** "fourty" in the typed list is corrected to "forty".
- **Tone marks.** Most forms have none. Add them before teaching pronunciation.
- **Site chat and Academy.** When the AI assistant or the Academy service is offline, the site uses
  these forms too. The old built-in lists (which gave, for example, "Ovbokhan" for 1) have been
  removed.
- **Audio.** Pronunciation recordings will come from the project owner.
