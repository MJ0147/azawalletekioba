# Obas of Benin: Benin Royal Museum Catalogue

The kings (Obas) of Benin shown in EKIOBA's Benin Royal Museum (`/museum`), in reign order,
from Oba Eweka I to Oba Ewuare II, the current Oba (reigning since 2016): 38 portraits in five eras.

## About this catalogue

- **Source.** Each Oba's name, reign and description are transcribed from the inscription plaque
  on his portrait. The dates and descriptions are as printed and have not been checked against
  historical sources.
- **Dates.** "c." (circa) marks an approximate date. Some spans run past the reign itself; for
  example, Ovonramwen's "1888–1914" includes his exile after 1897.
- **Portraits.** The portraits are contemporary artistic renderings in the Benin bronze style, not
  historical artefacts.
- **Gap.** There is no portrait of Oba Akenzua I (c. 1713–1735), so the catalogue goes from
  Oba Ozuere straight to Oba Eresoyen.
- **Numbers.** Catalogue numbers (No. 01–38) follow reign order and match the museum page.

## Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)

### No. 12 — Oba Ewuare the Great (1440–1473)

Expanded the empire, fortified Benin City, and established major royal arts and coral regalia traditions.

- Reign: 1440–1473
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-ewuare-the-great.jpg`

### No. 13 — Oba Ezoti (c. 1473)

Reigned only 14 days; assassinated by poisoned arrow at coronation.

- Reign: c. 1473
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-ezoti.jpg`

### No. 14 — Oba Olua (c. 1473–1480)

Son of Ewuare; known for heavy expenditures and links to early Itsekiri leadership.

- Reign: c. 1473–1480
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-olua.jpg`

### No. 15 — Oba Ozolua (c. 1480–1504)

Warrior king who greatly expanded Benin territory through military campaigns.

- Reign: c. 1480–1504
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-ozolua.jpg`

### No. 16 — Oba Esigie (c. 1504–1550)

Strengthened Portuguese trade and diplomatic contacts; era of Queen Mother Idia.

- Reign: c. 1504–1550
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-esigie.jpg`

### No. 17 — Oba Orhogbua (c. 1550–1578)

Portuguese-educated; established Eko (Lagos) military camp and installed the first Oba of Lagos.

- Reign: c. 1550–1578
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-orhogbua.jpg`

### No. 18 — Oba Ehengbuda (c. 1578–1606)

Last great warrior king; expanded territory and fixed the Benin–Oyo boundary at Otun.

- Reign: c. 1578–1606
- Era: Warrior Kings and Empire (1440–1606)
- Portrait: `frontend/static/images/museum/oba-ehengbuda.jpg`
