# Edo Alphabet

The Edo (Bini) alphabet, letter by letter, for the EKIOBA Edo Language Academy. Transcribed from
photos of an alphabet chart (two pages) supplied by the project owner. The numbers have their own
page: [edo-numbers.md](edo-numbers.md).

For each letter, the chart gives a word from English (or another language) with a similar sound,
with the matching letters highlighted. It then gives an Edo word with that letter, and the meaning.
In the tables below, the highlighted letters are in **bold**.

## Notes

- **Missing rows.** The O and Ọ rows are not in either photo; they fall between Ṅ and P.
- **Inferred letters.** The second page's letter column is cut off in the photo, so its letters are
  inferred from the highlighted sounds. The letters Ȳ and Ỹ are inferred from the Edo words,
  which use *ȳ* (the sound in "garage") and *ỹ* (the Spanish *ñ*).
- **J.** The chart gives no Edo word for J. Its example, *Ojukwu*, is an Igbo name.
- **Sounds-like column.** Examples are as printed, including "Elefant". "to chose" in the Z row is
  corrected to "to choose".
- **Words already in the Academy vocabulary:**
  - *Ebe* is already listed; its meaning is now "book / leaf / paper".
  - *Ẹghẹ* 'time' is already listed as *eghe* in the everyday vocabulary.
  - *tota* 'to sit' is already listed as *tótà* 'sit down'.
  - *Ẹỹẹn* 'snake' is already listed as *Ẹyẹn* in the animal vocabulary, spelled with a plain *y*.
  - *goh* 'to scream / to shout' is probably the same verb as *gó* 'shout' in the adjectives list;
    both are kept.
  - *Iȳe* 'mother': the everyday vocabulary spells grandmother *iye nokhua*, with a plain *y*.
- **Tone marks.** Few words on the chart carry them. Add them before teaching pronunciation.
- **Audio.** Pronunciation recordings will come from the project owner.
