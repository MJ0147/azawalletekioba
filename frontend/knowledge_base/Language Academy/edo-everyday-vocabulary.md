# Edo Everyday Vocabulary

Everyday Edo (Bini) words and phrases for the EKIOBA Edo Language Academy: family and people,
home and school, food, the sky, time, and useful phrases. Entries are recorded as supplied by
the project owner. Tone marks were not supplied.

"Already listed" means the Academy vocabulary already has the word, usually with tone marks.
That entry is kept, and no untoned duplicate is added.

## Notes

- **Grandparents.** Both were supplied with a separator ("erha – nokhua", "iye-nokhua") and are
  stored as two-word phrases. *Erha* and *iye* on their own were not supplied, so they are not
  listed separately.
- **Tone marks.** Add them before teaching pronunciation.
- **Casing.** Some words were supplied capitalised (*Ikpia, Okhuo, Igho, Iso*). Edo nouns need no
  capital, so the Academy vocabulary stores them in lowercase.
