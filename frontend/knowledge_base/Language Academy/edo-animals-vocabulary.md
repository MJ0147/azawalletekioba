Language Academy. Entries are recorded as supplied; spelling and casing are unchanged.

## Notes

- **Snake / cobra:** supplied as "Snake: Ẹyẹn / Ẹbi (Cobra)". Read here as *Ẹyẹn* = snake
  (general) and *Ẹbi* = cobra. Confirm that reading.
- **Tone marks:** none of these entries have tone marks. The adjectives reference does. Add
  them before teaching pronunciation.
- **Casing:** *eghile*, *egui* and *emẹ* were supplied in lowercase, the rest capitalised. Edo
  nouns need no capital, so the academy dataset stores them all in lowercase.
- **Check with a native speaker:** *Ẹkiyhẹ* (frog) uses "yh", which is not one of the standard
  Edo digraphs (*vb, kp, gb, mw, rh, kh, gh*). It may be a typo.
- **Grouping:** snail and tortoise are listed under wild animals, and frog under birds and
  reptiles, because that is where they were supplied.
